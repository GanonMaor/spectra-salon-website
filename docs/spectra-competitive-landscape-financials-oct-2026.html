Read the metrics correctly

Revenue is company income. GMV and payments/bookings volume are customer transaction values processed on a platform and are not company revenue. “Incremental customer revenue” is value attributed to customer outcomes, not Zenoti sales. Private-company figures are only shown when sourced and dated.

Direct Competitors
Color & Cost Management

Direct competition at the color bar

Vish and SalonScale already measure real material use and formulas. Spectra’s differentiation cannot rely on data access alone; it must be demonstrated through workflow quality, accuracy, automation, and the decisions generated from that data.

Investor implication

These are not feature-less incumbents. Both already create formula and material-consumption datasets. Spectra must prove a faster stylist workflow, higher-quality structured data, stronger automation from treatment to inventory, and better decision support—not merely the existence of a scale or formula history.

Strategic read-through

Capital validates the category, but scale does not prove depth in color production. Fresha and Zenoti have the strongest current financial signals; Mindbody demonstrates a large historical outcome; Phorest demonstrates durable salon-specific expansion. Spectra’s opportunity is to turn measured treatment economics into a wedge for the broader platform.

Evidence Rules

Official / SEC

Company press releases, SEC filings and investor announcements. Strongest evidence for amount and date.

Filing-derived / reported

Structured databases or reputable reporting based on company accounts. Useful, but entity and currency must be checked.

Third-party estimate

Shown only when no company disclosure exists. Never presented as audited or official revenue.
